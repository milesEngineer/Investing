## Source claims (as captured)
### From video discussion
- AI trades always carry uncertainty.
- **LLY**
  - ~30% CAGR for ~10 years with relatively little mainstream attention (as-of capture date).
  - Framed as non-cyclical growth: obesity and diabetes medicines tied to quality and quantity of life; supply constraints noted.
  - Thesis that LLY could become the largest S&P name by 2030.
  - Contrast vs AI names with cyclical/multiple-expansion risk and terminal-value questions; obesity demand not seen as near-term disrupted; aging buyer base; demand financed in part by Medicare/Medicaid.
  - Relative preference for healthcare in prior stress regimes (dotcom, GFC).
- **MU**
  - Compared to LLY as a cash-cow story that also grew across adjacent segments.
  - Capture note expected further MU upside less likely as a full double from then-current levels; more like ~60–80% (market levels are as-of the note date, not advice).
- Longevity theme may have second-order demand (e.g. recreation up, some food categories down).

### From tulipking thread (LLY / Reta)
- Bull case discussed around multi-trillion outcome and large tradable size.
- Three-part framing captured:
  1. Drug demand still under-appreciated despite awareness of the drugs.
  2. Reta / social-dynamics and adjacent indications (e.g. sleep apnea, knee pain) expand demand; very large treated-population scenarios discussed.
  3. Clinical/regulatory moat and ability to fund compute (“AI compute cluster”) as additional narrative.
- Counter-arguments noted: Chinese peptide competition; AI-company entrants; high friction of injections limiting multi-product stacking.
- Timing comment: not in a hurry; limited downside argued via other drugs in the franchise.
